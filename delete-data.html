OctoGraph - Delete Your Data

OctoGraph stores all data locally on your device. No data is sent to or stored on our servers. You can delete all your data at any time using the steps below.

How to delete your data

1. Open OctoGraph on your device
2. Tap the Info tab
3. Scroll down to Legal & Privacy
4. Tap "Delete All My Data"
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What is deleted

Data typeAction
Cached energy consumption dataDeleted immediately
Cached rate and tariff dataDeleted immediately
Octopus Energy API keyDeleted immediately
Bright/Glow credentialsDeleted immediately
App preferences and settingsDeleted immediately
Debug log filesDeleted immediately

All data is removed from your device instantly. There is no retention period because no data is stored on our servers.

Alternative: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling OctoGraph from your device will also remove all locally stored data.
