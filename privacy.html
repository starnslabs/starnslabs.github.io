OctoGraph Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 March 2026

Who We Are

OctoGraph is developed by Starns Labs. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Contact: starnslabs@gmail.com

Data We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal data on our servers. OctoGraph has no backend server — all processing happens on your device.

Data Stored On Your Device

  • Octopus Energy API key — entered by you, stored locally to fetch your usage data
  • Energy consumption data — cached locally for performance (electricity and gas usage)
  • Tariff and rate information — fetched from Octopus Energy API
  • Bright/Glow API credentials — if you use the smart meter feature
  • App preferences — display settings, Pro unlock status

This data never leaves your device except when making API requests to the services listed below.

Third-Party Services

OctoGraph connects to the following external services:

  • Octopus Energy API (api.octopus.energy) — to fetch your energy usage, rates, and tariff information. Authenticated with your API key.
  • Bright/Glow API (api.glowmarkt.com) — optional, for real-time smart meter data. Authenticated with your Bright account credentials.
  • Met Office DataHub (data.hub.api.metoffice.gov.uk) — weather forecast data for energy cost predictions. Your configured latitude and longitude (approximate location) are sent as query parameters to retrieve local forecasts.
  • Elexon BMRS (data.elexon.co.uk) — wholesale energy price data. No personal data is sent.

Advertising (Free Tier Only)

The free version of OctoGraph displays ads powered by Unity Ads. Unity Technologies may collect:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

You are asked for consent before any ad data is collected. You can choose:

  • Personalised ads — ad partners use device data for relevant ads
  • Non-personalised ads — limited data collection, generic ads
  • No ads — upgrade to OctoGraph Pro to remove all advertising and tracking

Unity Ads privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Your Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access — all your data is stored locally on your device; you can view it at any time
  • Deletion — use "Delete All My Data" on the Info tab to clear all cached data, credentials, and preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes app-private storage, though Android widget data may persist until the widget is removed from the home screen
  • Withdraw consent — tap "Change Ad Preferences" on the Info tab at any time to change or withdraw your advertising consent
  • Portability — your energy data originates from Octopus Energy; you can export it from their platform
  • Object — you can opt out of personalised advertising at any time

Data Security

All API communications use HTTPS encryption. Your API keys are stored in the app's private storage area, accessible only to OctoGraph. We do not transmit your credentials to any service other than the ones listed above.

Children's Privacy

OctoGraph is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
