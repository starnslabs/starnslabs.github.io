Pioneered Unity3D for automotive HMI saving £150K/year. CAR GUARDIAN, CAR SHIELD, VAN SENTINEL cybersecurity suite. Carbon fibre low-floor REEV bus. Ford Edge REV. Jaguar XJL EV. Metrovan REEV. Hardened Android with SE Linux.

Morgan EV3 and EV4. Amazon 7.5T REEV truck. Perdana luxury limo. BattScope and VodaScope 3D VR data visualisation. ISO 26262 functional safety adoption, safety and quality teams built.

Approach

AI-Augmented Engineering

I use AI every single day. It's a genuine force-multiplier — I've watched it find 35 bugs in a single session that would have shipped, architect systems in minutes that would take days to plan, and turn a one-person team into something that punches far above its weight.

But I treat AI exactly like a brilliant intern: capable, fast, and occasionally confidently wrong. You wouldn't let an intern loose on production code without testing and peer review — and you shouldn't let AI either.

Every line AI generates goes through the same rigour as human code: dry-run testing, data-path tracing, edge-case analysis, and 40 years of engineering instinct asking "does that actually look right?" The result is the speed of AI with the reliability of proper engineering discipline.
