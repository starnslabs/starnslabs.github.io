Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 April 2026

Who We Are

Family Estate Legacy Planner (FELP) is developed and published by Starns Labs Ltd, registered in England.

Contact: support@starnslabs.ltd

Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on our servers. Full stop.

FELP has no backend server, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no internet connection requirement. The app works entirely offline.

Data Stored On Your Device

All data you enter into FELP is stored exclusively on your device using your browser's local storage (localStorage). This includes:

  • Personal and family details — names, dates of birth, addresses, contact information
  • Legal documents — will details, LPA information, solicitor contacts
  • Medical information — GP details, conditions, medications, end-of-life wishes
  • Financial information — property details, bank accounts, pensions, insurance
  • Passwords and credentials — encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a master password you set
  • Photographs — stored locally as base64 data within the app's storage
  • App preferences — family name, active tab, last saved timestamp

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server, cloud service, or third party. When you use the Export or Report functions, files are created locally on your device or shared via your device's native sharing mechanism — we have no access to these files.

Third-Party Services

None. FELP does not connect to any external servers, APIs, or third-party services. The app makes zero network requests.

Advertising

None. FELP contains no advertisements and no ad-tracking code.

Encryption

The Passwords section of FELP uses AES-256-GCM encryption via the Web Crypto API built into your device's browser engine. Your master password is used to derive an encryption key — we never see or store this password. If you forget your master password, encrypted data cannot be recovered.

Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access — all your data is stored on your device and visible within the app at all times
  • Portability — use the Export function to download all your data as a JSON file at any time
  • Deletion — use "Clear All Data & Reset Planner" in Settings to permanently delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data
  • Rectification — you can edit any field at any time directly within the app

Since we do not collect or process any of your data, there is no data held by us to request, modify, or delete.

Data Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage area on your device, accessible only to FELP. We strongly recommend:

  • Using the Export function regularly to create backup copies of your data
  • Storing exported JSON files securely (e.g. encrypted USB drive, password-protected cloud storage)
  • Changing the default master password in the Passwords section immediately
  • Using your device's built-in security features (screen lock, biometrics)

Children's Privacy

FELP is not directed at children under 13. The app is designed for adults planning family estate matters.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the app's functionality changes. Updates will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
